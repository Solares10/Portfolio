Steady State Distributions of Generalized Birth-Death Chains

Mathematics honors project, team of five, Cal Poly Pomona

Our team studied the space of all 3-state birth-death Markov chains with nonzero transition probabilities. We worked out how a chain’s parameters determine its steady-state distribution, grouped chains that share the same steady state, and studied the topology of the resulting space. Along the way we split the problem into cases using eigenvalues and used a dual matrix to connect steady states to the gambler’s ruin problem.

Our main conjecture is that the space of these equivalence classes is homeomorphic to the 2-dimensional probability simplex without its boundary.

  • Markov chains
  • Probability
  • Linear algebra
  • Topology
  • Research poster
